Privacy and your data

Your requests, results, and secrets stay on your computer. Here is where they live, how secrets are protected, and the little the app ever sends.

Everything stays on your computer

Requests are sent from your computer straight to the API you test — that's what lets the app reach internal and VPN-only APIs. There is no APITestCenter server in between, no account, and nothing about your requests, answers, or results is uploaded.

Where your files are

Saved requests, environments, collection run orders, and history are plain files in one folder:

SystemFolder
Windows (installer or portable)%APPDATA%\ApiTestCenter
Windows (Microsoft Store version)Documents\APITestCenter
macOS~/Library/Application Support/ApiTestCenter
Linux~/.local/share/apitestcenter

About → Open folder opens it. You can back it up, copy it to another computer, or keep saved requests in a Git repository for CI.

Secrets

Tokens, passwords, and API keys — in saved requests and in secret environment variables — are never written into those files. They are encrypted with a key your operating system protects (Windows DPAPI, macOS Keychain, Linux keyring), so another user of the computer cannot read them. Exports never contain them either; auth appears as a type only.

Values saved from answers (Save values) are kept in memory until you close the app.

What the app sends — the complete list

  1. Your requests, to the APIs you choose to test.
  2. An update check (installed Windows app and Linux AppImage): it downloads a small file from GitHub describing the latest version. Nothing about you or your tests is sent.
  3. Usage counts — only if you say yes. Once a day: how many requests, load tests, compares, and imports you ran, plus the app version and operating system, with a random install number. Never URLs, results, names, or anything you type. Say no and nothing is counted or sent; change your mind any time in Help.

Details, and the exact data: apitestcenter.com/privacy.
