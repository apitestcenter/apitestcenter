Signing in and your plan

Signing in is optional. Why you might, how it works with an emailed code, your computers, a Team's people, and deleting your account.

You don't need an account

Everything in the app works without signing in. Your requests, environments and results stay on your computer either way — signing in never uploads them.

You'd sign in to:

  • use a Pro or Team plan without copying a license key around;
  • see and sign out the computers your plan is used on;
  • manage the people on a Team you bought.

Sign in

In the app: Sign in at the top right (or Settings → Plan). On the website: apitestcenter.com/account.html.

  1. Type your email and click Email me a code. There's no password.
  2. Type the 6-digit code from the email (it lasts 10 minutes) and click Sign in.

Use the email you paid with, or the one your Team admin added. The app checks your plan about twice a day; offline, it keeps the plan it last saw.

Your computers

A plan works on up to 3 computers at once. Settings → Plan and the account page list them by name, with when each was last used, and Sign out on each.

Signing in on a fourth computer asks which one to sign out first. Signing in on the website doesn't count as a computer.

Your work on more than one computer

Your account carries your plan, not your work: requests and results are never sent to us, so they don't sync on their own. To use the same requests on two computers, keep the project in a folder both can reach:

  1. On the first computer: Home → New project → Choose… and pick a folder inside OneDrive, Dropbox or Google Drive — or a Git repository.
  2. On the other computer, once the folder has synced: Home → Open folder… and pick the same folder.

Two things to know:

  • Secrets stay on each computer. Tokens and passwords are encrypted with a key only that computer has (Privacy and your data), so type them once on each computer.
  • Use a project on one computer at a time. Editing on two at once can leave the sync service with two copies of a file. With Git, pull before you start and push when you're done.

A Team

The person who buys a Team chooses who gets its seats: on the account page, Your Team → Add with each person's work email. A Team for 5 is 5 people. Using the app yourself? Add yourself too — it takes a seat.

Each person gets an email saying what to do: download the app and sign in with that email. Remove someone and their app goes back to Free within a day.

Not on your company's Team yet? Sign in on the account page and use Ask for access with your admin's email.

Delete your account

On the account page: Delete my account. It removes your email and signs out every computer. What you made in the app stays on your computers. If you pay for a plan, cancel it first with Manage subscription.

We keep only your email and the names of the computers you sign in from. Payments and invoices are with Paddle, our reseller. Details: apitestcenter.com/privacy.
